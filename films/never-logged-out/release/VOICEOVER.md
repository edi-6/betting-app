# THE PLAYER WHO NEVER LOGGED OUT: voice-over script

These are his 72 lines, timed to the film. `voiceover.srt` has the same timing, so it can be imported into any editor
as a guide track or uploaded to YouTube as captions. The film is delivered twice: with the lines burned in as
subtitles (it works with no voice at all), and clean, with no subtitles, for your own voice and captions.

The sound mix already leaves room for a voice: the ambience and music dip by about 3 dB under every line.

## How he should sound

He is a real person playing a game alone at 4 AM, not a narrator. Keep it close to the microphone and quiet. He
never screams. The fear is in how quiet he gets. His arc:

- **curious and amused** (acts 0–1),
- **confused** (act 2),
- **obsessed** (acts 4–5),
- **paranoid** (act 6),
- **genuinely terrified** (acts 7–9).

From act 3 on, most lines are close to a whisper.
