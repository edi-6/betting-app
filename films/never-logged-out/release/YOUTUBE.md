## Description
```
I found this world on an old computer. Last played: 1 day ago.

It shouldn't exist. The account hasn't logged in since 2018.

Everything in this video is in the world. Pause it.

04:04
```
- Keep it short. Don't explain anything, and leave out the chapters for the first few days: they spoil the pacing and
  the retention curve.
- The "04:04" at the end is a clue anchor: every timestamp in the film is 04:04.
