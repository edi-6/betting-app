# ARG clue guide (spoilers: for you, not for the description)

## What's "really" going on
This is never stated in the film, but every clue is consistent with it.
- A world needs two players: one plays it, one watches.
- NOAH_404 was a player. On day 19 he found out what the world wants, and he became the watcher. He is
  "underneath" it now.
- The world copies everything one day ahead. The copies are dated with the day each player arrived:

  | Player | Arrived |
  | ------ | ------- |
  | PLAYER_1 | 2018-06-14 (Noah) |
  | PLAYER_2 | 2020-11-03 |
  | PLAYER_3 | 2025-07-01 |
  | PLAYER_4 | 2026-04-04, tomorrow, the protagonist |

- The figure with his skin is the world's copy of him. When he turns around, the copy takes over the playing and he
  becomes the watcher ("You stayed.").
- The ending points at the viewer. His own launcher account was **PLAYER_2**, and the last card says **PLAYER 2 IS
  ONLINE**. Is he the next watcher, or are you?

Numbers that recur: **04:04** and **404** (every timestamp), **19** (days, houses), **2** (players).
