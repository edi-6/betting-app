# Privacy Policy — BetLedger

**Last updated: 11 September 2026**

## The short version

BetLedger collects nothing. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Every
bet, transaction and setting you enter is stored only on your own device.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal
information, usage data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers or location
data.

## What is stored, and where

Everything you type into BetLedger — bets, bookmakers, stakes, notes, tags,
transactions and preferences — is written to your device's local application
storage. It never leaves the device unless you deliberately export it.

Deleting the app removes this data permanently. You can also erase it at any time
from **Settings → Your data → Erase all data**.

## Exports

The export features (CSV and JSON) write a file and hand it to your operating
system's standard share sheet. Where that file goes — email, a cloud drive, another
app — is entirely your choice, and is governed by whichever service you send it to.
BetLedger does not upload exports anywhere.

## Network use

BetLedger makes no network requests. It has no backend, syncs nothing, and works
fully offline.

The single exception is the "Find support" link on the Settings screen, which opens
<https://www.begambleaware.org/> in your browser. Following that link is your
choice, and BeGambleAware's own privacy policy applies once you do.

## Third-party services

There are none. No analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no advertising network, no
social login.

## Children

BetLedger is not directed at children and should not be used by anyone below the
legal gambling age in their jurisdiction.
