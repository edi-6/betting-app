# 50 Countries Battle Royale: Last One Inside Wins (YouTube Shorts)

Something completely different for the channel: no Minecraft, a **countryball battle royale**. Fifty countries,
each a glossy flag ball with cartoon eyes, bounce around inside a spinning neon ring that has a gap in it. Any ball
that falls out through the gap is eliminated. **The last one inside wins.**

It's built around the two things that make this kind of Short spread:

* **Everyone has a team.** *FIND YOUR COUNTRY* is the first thing on screen, and a board of all 50 flags under the
  ring shows every country's fate the whole time. When a ball goes out it flies down into its tile, and the tile
  shows the place it finished (#50, #49, ...). Viewers stay to see how far their country gets, and at the end the
  board is a full ranking to argue about in the comments.
* **A ball game that never stops moving.** The balls bounce like superballs from the first frame (ten countries
  are out in the first three seconds), the ring speeds up at TOP 10, and the survivors grow as the field thins,
  until two big balls are left in the ring for the final.

The match itself is real physics. In the one chosen here, the final is **Argentina vs France**, a rematch of the
2022 World Cup final, and Argentina wins again. That's ready-made fuel for the comments.

1. **The hook.** All 50 balls packed in the middle of the ring: *FIND YOUR COUNTRY*, **50 LEFT**. On the first beat
   they burst apart, and Ireland is out within a third of a second.
2. **The cull.** The ring's gap sweeps round, and every time it passes the bottom a wave of balls drops out. Each one
   that goes whistles out of the ring with its name and place over it, curves down into its tile and pops. The
   counter ticks down, and every hard bounce off the ring rings a note.
3. **TOP 10.** *SPEED UP!* The ring spins faster and the music drops into its bigger second half.
4. **TOP 5, FINAL 3, FINAL 2.** *1 VS 1*: **ARGENTINA VS FRANCE**. The music strips back to a heartbeat and a
   ticking clock. Every bounce off the ring now plays the next note of a climbing motif, and there are near misses at
   the gap.
5. **The end.** Slow motion, the camera pushes in, France slips out through the gap, flash: **ARGENTINA WINS!** The
   winner glides to the middle with a big grin, a crown drops on its head, confetti rains down, the board lights up
   its #1, and *COMMENT YOUR COUNTRY*.

## Why this format

* Bouncing-ball Shorts ("will the ball escape?", balls in rotating rings) became one of the platform's biggest
  formats from late 2023, with single Shorts passing tens of millions of views
  ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/will-the-ball-escape-ball-bouncing-brainrot)). The channel's
  own [`../ball-escape`](../ball-escape) used it with a single ball.
* Countryballs (flags drawn as balls with eyes) are a long-running internet art style with a huge following
  ([Wikipedia: Polandball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polandball)).
* Putting the two together gives everyone in the world a team to root for, and the result is something to argue
  about: country rivalries and "where's my country?" fill the comments, which is what Shorts rewards. Wordless
  Shorts with a "wait for it" payoff also travel across languages
  ([air.io](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/13-types-of-shorts-that-always-go-viral-with-examples),
  [Epidemic Sound](https://www.epidemicsound.com/blog/youtube-trends-2026/)).

## How it's made

| | |
| --- | --- |
| The flags | `src/flags.py`: all 50 flags drawn in code from their construction sheets (stripe ratios, the Union Jack's counterchanged saltires, the Turkish and Pakistani crescents, China's stars turned to the big one, the US's 50 stars, the Korean taegeuk and trigrams, Canada's 11-point maple leaf, South Africa's Y, Brazil's globe with its band and motto...) and supersampled 4x. The coats of arms of Mexico, Spain and Portugal, Egypt's eagle, Kenya's shield and Argentina's sun are simplified but keep their layout and colours, and Brazil's 27 stars are placed by eye (Spica alone above the band). `python flags.py` draws a review sheet with each flag next to its emoji |
| The balls | `src/balls.py`: each flag wrapped onto a sphere (an elliptical disc-to-square mapping, so the whole flag shows and its edges wrap round the sides), shaded with a soft key light, a highlight and a dark countryball outline. Countryball eyes, white with no pupils, look where the ball is going, blink, widen in panic near the gap, turn to x x when it's out and ^ ^ for the winner |
| The physics | `src/sim.py`: pymunk (Chipmunk2D). The ring is a kinematic body with a gap, spinning at about 1 rad/s (1.45x from TOP 10). Under gravity the balls would settle, so a thermostat keeps each ball's energy at the same level and they bounce like superballs to the end. Each elimination makes the survivors grow (radius ~ (50/n)^0.33) and the gap with them. The first frame is a hexagonal pack that bursts outward. 640 matches were simulated and scored for pace and drama (a first elimination in under a second, no long lulls, a final 1 vs 1 of several seconds with near misses); this is seed 1485 |
| The edit | `src/main.py`: quiet stretches run up to 1.3x faster, the last elimination plays in slow motion (0.26x) with the camera pushing in on the gap and the board dimming, and a short shake marks every elimination |
| The picture | `src/render.py`, `src/hud.py`: a neon ring with bloom that pulses when it's hit, changes colour as the field shrinks (cyan, violet, magenta, red for the final, gold for the winner) and throws sparks off hard bounces; neon trails behind the last three; balls that squash on impact and lean into their motion; eliminated balls curving into their tile with a pop in their flag's colours; the counter, the name and place of each country going out, the TOP 10 / TOP 5 / FINAL banners, the head-to-head, and the winner's crown, light rays and confetti |
| Sound | `src/audio.py` (toolkit in `src/sound.py`): an original track at 128 BPM in A minor (four-on-the-floor kick, claps, a sidechained bass, a plucked arpeggio, a hook; wide saw chords from TOP 10), a riser and a hit at every milestone, a heartbeat, a clock and a drone for the final two, an A major fanfare for the winner with applause and party poppers. The effects come from the physics: a marimba note for each hard bounce off the ring, pitched by where on the ring it hits (low at the bottom, high at the top); in the final, the next note of a climbing motif for every bounce; knocks between balls; a whistle for each country going out, a little higher each time; a thunk and a ding as it lands on the board |

## Render it yourself

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
cd src
python flags.py ../preview/flags.png                 # the flags next to their emoji, to check them
python sim.py search 640 1000                        # simulate 640 matches and rank them (about 11 minutes)
python sim.py run 1485                               # record the chosen one
python main.py --out ../output                       # render (about 10 minutes on 4 cores), sound, encode
python main.py --out ../preview --stills 0,1300,2200 # a few frames as PNG
python qa.py ../output                               # checks and a contact sheet
python thumbnail.py ../output/thumbnail.jpg          # the cover; --no-title for a clean one
```

## Upload tips

* Upload the MP4 as it is: 60 fps, 9:16, under a minute, so YouTube treats it as a Short.
* There's one soft flash, when the winner is decided. Nothing strobes.
* Reply to the comments asking for a country: "it's in the next one". A Round 2 with the most-requested countries
  (and the same 50 back for a rematch) is the obvious follow-up, and the code takes a different seed to make a new
  match in minutes.
