# 1000 Balls... Watch the Shape (YouTube Shorts)

A new format for the channel: a **Galton board**. A thousand glowing balls stream down through 14 rows of pegs,
bouncing left or right at random at every one, and pile up in 15 rainbow bins. Out of all that randomness the pile
grows into a perfect **bell curve**. At the end the true curve draws itself over it: *THE BELL CURVE, EVERY SINGLE
TIME*. Every peg hit plays a note (higher toward the right), so the board sounds like a cascading music box over a
soft beat.

**Why it works:** "watch the shape" makes people stay to see it form, the reveal makes them say "wait, WHAT", and it's
the kind of video people send to friends and watch again.

**Ready to upload:** [`release/bell-curve.mp4`](release/bell-curve.mp4) (1080x1920, 60 fps, about 46 s, -14 LUFS),
cover [`release/thumbnail.jpg`](release/thumbnail.jpg).
