# Stare at the Red Dot for 20 Seconds... (YouTube Shorts)

A totally different format for the channel: an **optical illusion**. A slowly turning, expanding spiral with a red
dot in the middle and a 20-second countdown. When it hits zero, the video cuts to a still of the golden Sift valley
(from [`../sift-flight`](../sift-flight)), and the still picture seems to breathe and shrink. That's the motion
aftereffect, the "waterfall illusion": your eyes have adapted to the motion. Then come the prompts: *NOW LOOK AT YOUR
HAND* and *DID IT MOVE?* It works on your hand, your room, anything.

**Why it works:** the instruction is the hook (*stare at the red dot for 20 seconds*), the countdown holds viewers
to the end, and the payoff makes people rewatch, comment ("my hand MELTED") and send it to friends.
